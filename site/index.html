What it does

The security checkup your PC never had

Antivirus catches viruses. SentySurf catches everything around them – the settings, scams and slow changes attackers rely on – and explains each one so anyone can fix it.

☎

Scam Guard

Warns you the moment AnyDesk, TeamViewer or another remote-control tool starts, or someone connects to your screen – the #1 trick in tech-support scams.

✓

Security score

Checks firewall, antivirus, encryption, Windows updates, accounts and more, gives you a score, and a Fix it button for each problem.

◎

Change monitoring

Remembers what “normal” looks like and alerts you when new startup programs, services, scheduled tasks or open ports appear.

↻

App updates

Finds out-of-date apps and updates them in one click – or skip a version and get reminded later.

🧩

Browser extension review

Lists every extension in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox, flags search hijackers and spyware, and opens the right page to remove them.

◉

Camera & microphone alerts

Tells you whenever an app turns on your camera or microphone, and which app it was.

🌐

Hijack watch

Spots changed DNS or proxy settings and new homepages or startup pages – signs of malware redirecting your browsing.

✱

Password check

Find out if a password has leaked in a data breach. The password never leaves your PC.

⛨

Read-only by design

SentySurf never changes, deletes or ends anything unless you click a button that clearly says so. It runs alongside your antivirus.

Family

Look after the people who call you for computer help

Install SentySurf on your parents', kids' or grandparents' PCs and see every computer's security on one page. Get a notification the moment one of them needs you – like a scammer connecting with AnyDesk.

  • Join with a family code – no accounts, no shared folders
  • End-to-end encrypted with your code: nobody else can read it, not even us
  • Only a security summary is shared – never files, browsing or anything private
  • Inventory of every PC: Windows version, model, memory, disk – export to Excel
