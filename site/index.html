  1. Verdict firstThe header says whether anything needs you, and why, before you read a single number.

02 Capabilities

The coverage antivirus leaves out.

Antivirus inspects files. SentySurf watches the system around them: configuration, persistence, remote access, the network and the software you expose. It runs alongside Microsoft Defender.

Detect

  1. 01

    Remote-access sessions

    AnyDesk, TeamViewer or Quick Assist launching or accepting a connection. Built for the tech-support scam.

  2. 02

    Baseline drift

    New startup entries, services, scheduled tasks, listening ports and changes to sensitive files.

  3. 03

    Traffic redirection

    Altered DNS, proxy settings and homepage overrides, the usual signs of a hijack.

  4. 04

    Clipboard hijacking

    A copied crypto wallet address swapped for an attacker's, flagged within a second.

Harden

  1. 05

    Posture scoring

    Defender, firewall, BitLocker, UAC, Remote Desktop, automatic sign-in and backups, rescanned daily.

  2. 06

    Vulnerable software

    Missing Windows security updates, out-of-support software and exploitable app versions, patched in place through winget.

  3. 07

    Ransomware folders

    Windows' Controlled Folder Access, switched on and managed without digging through Defender.

  4. 08

    Home network

    Every device on your Wi-Fi, new arrivals flagged, router checked for UPnP and Telnet.

Inspect

  1. 09

    Links before you click

    Look-alike domains, foreign look-alike letters, hidden redirects and risky downloads.

  2. 10

    Files before you open

    Disguised extensions, macros and broken signatures, plus a Microsoft Defender scan.

  3. 11

    Browser extensions

    A 0–100 risk score per extension, permissions in plain language, policy-forced installs caught.

  4. 12

    Camera & microphone

    Each app that turns them on, straight from Windows' own access records.

Connect

  1. 13

    SIEM forwarding

    Alerts and scan results to Splunk, Sentinel, Elastic, QRadar or Wazuh: Event Log, syslog with CEF, HTTPS or JSON.

  2. 14

    Family console

    Every PC you look after on one screen, end-to-end encrypted with a family code.

  3. 15

    macOS

    FileVault, Gatekeeper, SIP, XProtect, sharing services and unsupported macOS versions.

  4. 16

    Browser check

    A free web check of any browser's version, WebRTC exposure, tracker blocking and fingerprint. Try it

03 Remote access

Stops the tech-support scam at the first connection.

Scammers talk people into installing a remote-control tool, then take over the screen. SentySurf flags the tool the moment it launches and raises a high-severity alert as soon as someone connects, on your PC or any PC in your family.

04 Network

Know every device on your Wi-Fi.

One scan lists the phones, TVs and laptops on your network. From then on SentySurf notices newcomers on its own, without probing anything, and checks the router for settings botnets abuse.

  • Name devices and mark the ones you know
  • Alerts when an unknown device joins
  • UPnP and Telnet exposure on the router

05 Safety check

A second opinion before you click.

Paste a link from a text or email and SentySurf explains what's wrong with it without opening it. Pick a download and it checks who signed it, where it came from and what's hiding inside.

  • paypa1.com and rnicrosoft.com caught as look-alikes
  • invoice.pdf.exe caught as a disguised program
  • Password breach check using k-anonymity

06 Family

Look after the PCs you're responsible for.

Enrol a parent's laptop or a child's desktop with a 16-character family code. Each one reports its status to your dashboard, and you're told the moment one needs you.

  • No accounts and no shared folders
  • End-to-end encrypted with a key derived from the code
  • Status summaries only: never files or browsing
  • Hardware inventory for every PC, exportable to CSV

07 Setup

Running in two minutes.

There's nothing to configure. Updates install themselves after their checksum is verified.

  1. Install

    Run the installer. Live monitoring starts for every account on the PC.

  2. Note your fingerprint

    SentySurf baselines the system and shows a 16-character fingerprint. If it ever changes without you, its records were tampered with.

  3. Fix and enrol

    Work through findings by severity, then add the other PCs you look after.
