Remote-access detection

Stops the tech-support scam at the first connection

Scammers talk victims into installing AnyDesk, TeamViewer or Quick Assist, then take over the screen. SentySurf flags the tool the moment it launches and raises a high-severity alert when an inbound session starts, on your PC or any PC in your family.

Baseline drift

Knows what normal looks like, and what changed

On install SentySurf records a known-good baseline of startup entries, services, scheduled tasks, listening ports, Defender exclusions, WMI subscriptions and sensitive files. Any deviation is reported with its signature status, so a new unsigned autorun never goes unnoticed.

Capabilities

The coverage antivirus leaves out

Antivirus inspects files. SentySurf inspects the system around them: configuration, persistence, remote access and the software you expose. Every finding carries a severity rating and a remediation step.

Remote-access detection

Posture scoring

Audits firewall, Defender, BitLocker, Windows Update, UAC and local administrator accounts. Weighted by severity, scored out of 100, with one-click remediation where Windows allows it.

Baseline drift

Records a known-good baseline, then reports new startup entries, services, scheduled tasks, listening ports and changes to sensitive files.

↻

Patch management

Inventories outdated applications through winget and updates them in place. Skip a release or defer it when the timing is wrong.

🧩

Extension audit

Enumerates extensions across Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox, rates their permissions and marks search hijackers for removal.

◉

Camera & microphone access

Logs each application that opens the camera or microphone, straight from Windows' own access records.

🌐

Traffic redirection

Detects altered DNS resolvers, proxy settings and homepage overrides, the standard signs of traffic being rerouted.

✱

Credential exposure

Tests a password against known breach data using k-anonymity. The password itself never leaves the device.

⛨

Read-only by design

Observes and reports. Nothing is changed, quarantined or terminated without an explicit action from you. Runs alongside Defender.

Family

One console for every PC you're responsible for

Enrol a parent's laptop or a child's desktop with a family code. Each device reports its security status to your dashboard, and you're alerted the moment one needs you, such as a remote session opening on a grandparent's PC.

  • Enrol with a 16-character family code. No accounts, no shared folders.
  • End-to-end encrypted with a key derived from that code. The relay only ever holds ciphertext.
  • Status summaries only. No files, browsing history or personal data.
  • Hardware and OS inventory for every device, exportable to CSV.
Deployment
