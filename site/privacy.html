Privacy

SentySurf runs locally. There is no account, no advertising and no telemetry. This page sets out what it reads, what it stores and the small amount it sends over the network.

What SentySurf reads on your PC

Security settings, the list of running programs and their network connections, startup entries, scheduled tasks and services, browser extension lists, and Windows' own records of which apps used your camera or microphone, and the list of devices your PC already sees on your home network. Clipboard Guard looks at copied text only to see whether it's a crypto wallet address; it's never saved or sent. Files and links are inspected only when you choose them on the Safety check page. It never reads your documents, emails, messages or browsing history.

What it stores

Its baseline (the known-good snapshot of your system), alert history, settings, scan results and the names you give devices on your network, in a folder on your PC that only administrators can open (C:\ProgramData\SentySurf). Nothing is uploaded.

What it sends over the internet

  • Update check: every 6 hours SentySurf asks sentysurf.com and GitHub whether a newer release exists. It's an ordinary web request; nothing about you or your PC is included.
  • Password check (on the Safety check page, only when you use it): only the first 5 characters of a one-way fingerprint (SHA-1 hash) of the password go to Have I Been Pwned. The password itself never leaves your PC and is never saved.
  • App update check (during scans): Windows' built-in winget tool contacts Microsoft's app catalogue.
  • Network scan (only when you click Scan network): one empty packet to each address on your own home network so devices show up, plus a check of whether your router answers UPnP or Telnet. Nothing leaves your home network.
  • Safety check: links are checked on your PC and never opened or sent. Files are scanned by Microsoft Defender on your PC. “Look up on VirusTotal” opens your browser with the file's fingerprint (SHA-256) only, and only when you click it.
  • Family mode (only if you enable it): see below.

Family mode

Every 20 minutes, and immediately when its status changes, each enrolled PC shares a short security summary: PC name, score, issue counts, protection on/off, important alert titles, and basic facts for the family inventory (Windows version, maker and model, processor type, memory and free disk space).

The summary is encrypted on the PC with your family code before it's sent. The relay only ever sees scrambled text on a channel name that's also derived from your code, so nobody without the code can read it, including us.

The summary travels through SentySurf's own relay at relay.sentysurf.com (run on Cloudflare), with the public ntfy.sh service as a backup. Each relay keeps a summary for up to 12 hours and then deletes it. Like any web server, the relays briefly see the internet address a request came from; SentySurf doesn't store or use it. No files, browsing, camera/microphone activity, serial numbers, user names or addresses are ever included.

This website

sentysurf.com uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. The download buttons send you to the installer on GitHub, where SentySurf's releases are published.

Read-only by design

SentySurf never changes, deletes or ends anything on your computer unless you click a button that clearly says so (such as Update all or Fix it).
